Our Story

Delicious Bites is a cozy, family-owned restaurant founded in 2010. We're dedicated to serving fresh, flavorful meals with warm hospitality.

Founder: Priya Mehta

Priya is a passionate entrepreneur and food lover who founded Delicious Bites after traveling across Europe and India. She holds a degree in Hospitality Management from the University of Delhi and spent over a decade managing fine-dining restaurants in Mumbai and Singapore. Known for her leadership and empathy, Priya believes in sustainability and community-building through food.

Head Chef: Rajiv Kapoor

Chef Rajiv is a culinary genius with over 15 years of experience in international cuisine. Trained at Le Cordon Bleu in Paris, he has worked in Michelin-starred kitchens and specializes in fusing Indian flavors with global techniques. Known for his creativity, discipline, and perfectionism, Rajiv’s signature dishes are the heart of our menu.
